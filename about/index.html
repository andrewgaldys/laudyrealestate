I help clients turn a broad South Florida search into a property specific plan.

Local attention, property by property.

I work with buyers and sellers in Fort Lauderdale and the surrounding South Florida market through Fidelity Real Estate.

My focus includes waterfront homes, central Fort Lauderdale neighborhoods, oceanfront condominiums and investment or development opportunities. I enjoy the analysis behind a transaction: which comparable sales actually matter, what a property can become and which practical constraints change its value to a particular buyer.

For relocation clients, the work begins with the life you want here. For an owner considering a sale, it begins with a clear view of the property’s competing alternatives. The goal is a focused plan built around your address and priorities.
