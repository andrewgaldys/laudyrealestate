Bridges

A fixed bridge caps your boat’s height. A drawbridge only costs time.

  • 17th Street Causeway: 55 ft closed
  • New River rail bridge: 4 ft closed
  • Schedules: 33 CFR 117

Dockage & Depth

Frontage on the plat is not berth length.

  • Dock reach: 25 ft or 25% of the waterway
  • 60-ft canal: 15-ft dock
  • Depth at mean low water

Seawalls

A failing wall is priced at replacement cost.

  • 4 ft NAVD88 minimum before 2035
  • 5 ft by 2050
  • 50% repair triggers full compliance

Before You Buy

  • Boat specs in writing
  • Route map with every bridge
  • Survey with seawall cap height
  • Elevation certificate and flood zone
  • Depth soundings at low water
  • Marine contractor inspection
  • Seawall, dock and lift permits
  • Wind and flood insurance quotes
